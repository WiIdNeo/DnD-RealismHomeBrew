# The Abyss

The Abyss is a place deep under the surface. It is reaching the hot part of the deeps where lava is flowing and smelting the stone. But at some points like rifts in the surface it sometimes reaches up to it.
But the abyss is not only a place of fire and hot, you finde there swamps and oceans below the surface. And you find deserts and you find meadows powered by the warmth of the lava.
Also volcanos often feature a way directly into the Abyss.

The Abyss features a lot of ore and usefull stone, so humans try to get there to start building ab better walls and houses or smith weapons.

But the Abyss is holding dangers like the demons don't want the humans to steal their ore and Stone and kill them for their souls.

## Nine Hells

Only a small part of the nine hells, often close to surface is usable for humans as the demons living here do cantracts with them about greeding ore and giving safe passage. This is called the nine Hells.
