Demons (from greek Daimon) can translated as messanger of the gods. So they are basically the same as Angels. 
So those terms are mainly flavor of humanoids and the angels, who say they are the good people, because they serve the "good" gods.



# Angels

The Angels are the celestials of the 'good' gods, while they call the celestials serve the 'evil' gods to be demons. The angels are in a constant war about souls and conquer the demons and the devils.

# Demons

While Angels differ the serving celestials the demons do not really care about those topics. Only demons who where humanoids earlier call the angels weak fools and reflect this view of the world.

# Devils

Devils are a kind of not serving celestials. But as the serving celestials they gather souls to become more powerfull.

# Eladrin

Eladrin are non-serving eladrin as well, but different to devils they do not gather souls, as they understand the non-natural enthrophy of souls will result in death and devastation of the world. So they work together with some gods to keep this natural flow up. Those have nearly no connection to humanoids, only a small group fighting and thinking of balance in the world, like the followers of the philosophy of balance, also known as Ao's cult.
