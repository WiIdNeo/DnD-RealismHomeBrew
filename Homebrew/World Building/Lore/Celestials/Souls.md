# What is a soul

A soul is to understand like a specific spirit. It is more the energy which powers you up and like every energy it can't be destroyed or created.  

A soul is also not what defines a creature alone. Big part the information is saved in brain of the creature. This also makes it quite impossible to transfer the soul of someone to another body to revive him. Because of this you always need the corps to revive someone.

A soul behaves like a spirit floating in outside-world. They are called to land of never return naturally, but in different speed. The Souls don't know they are going somewhere, they just find the land of never return, they do not search it normally. Only strong feelings like hate, love and obsession, and important memories are contained in souls. So they may stay at a place to guard it not even notice the call of the land of never return. 

So as long as they are on the way of never-return they can be called or appear in dreams. But as they reach land without return they can only be memorized. And this is the task of Land without return. It makes the memories faith from others but also cleanses the soul from all feelings and memories to not more than energy. This normally takes hundreds or thousands of years or until all persons knew the person have died.

This also is a reason why many people fear the normal way of living, faithing and rebirth: They fear to leave the world resulting in many people try to contract a demon to become immortal and never faith. This is why many kings contract with gods: They want to make sure to be everlasting.
